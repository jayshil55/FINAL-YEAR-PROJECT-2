## Additional codebase observations

- `src/components/ui/GlassCard.jsx` exists but no usage/import was found in `src/`; treat as an unused design component unless later usage is added.
- `src/services/authService.ts` and `src/pages/auth/ForgotPassword.tsx` are not imported by the route tree. The live auth flows are the `.jsx` files and `AuthContext.jsx`; the `.tsx` files should not be treated as evidence of active behavior.
- `Login.jsx` duplicates signup controls implemented in `Signup.jsx`. Its “Remember me” input has no controlled state or persistence handler in the inspected form.
- Feature names, labels, and route text should be reconciled during redesign against these actual behaviors, especially “AI,” “personalized,” “history,” and “download.”
